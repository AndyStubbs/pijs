Sound Advanced

Listening demo for the sound-advanced plugin: synth() with filter, vibrato, tremolo, pulse duty, and arpeggio; periodic noise; presets; generateSfx(); PLAY instruments, synthesized and sampled; music sync with onPlay(); bus volume and effects; getSoundLevels(); and recording. Build with npm run build and serve the repository with npm run server.

Synth

Periodic noise uses the frequency as its clock rate; try values above 1000.

Presets

Generator

Seed 0 is the category's built-in preset. The same category and seed always give the same sound; variation nudges every parameter each time it plays.


	


	

		
Instruments

		
		
		
		
		
		
"Redefine while queued" starts a song with instrument 9, then redefines 9 as a
			quiet sine; the playing song keeps its sound, and the next click uses the new
			definition.

	


	

		
Sample instruments

		
		
		
		
		
Both files are made in this page and loaded with loadAudio(). Instrument
			10 plays a plucked string recorded at C4, so notes change its pitch and length
			together. Instrument 11 loops an organ tone whose root frequency, about 260.87 Hz,
			fits the loop exactly, with a little vibrato. "Play before the file loads" loads a
			new copy and plays at once: that song is silent, with one console warning, and a
			second song after ready() is heard.

	


	

		
Music sync

		
		
		
		
		
Each note flashes when it is heard, in a lane for its track, placed by pitch. The
			ring pulses on the first track's notes. Songs from the Instruments section sync
			too. Unchecking the box calls offPlay(); hiding the tab mid-song skips
			the late notes instead of flashing them all at once.

	


	

		
Buses

		

		
Bus volume follows the effect, so muting a bus also cuts its reverb or echo tail.

		

			
			
		

		
Moving the cutoff updates the chain in place: the filter ramps over 20 ms without
			clicks, and the reverb tail continues.
