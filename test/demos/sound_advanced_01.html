Sound Advanced

Listening demo for the sound-advanced plugin: synth() with filter, vibrato, tremolo, pulse duty, and arpeggio; periodic noise; presets; PLAY instruments; bus volume and effects; and getSoundLevels(). Build with npm run build and serve the repository with npm run server.

Synth

Periodic noise uses the frequency as its clock rate; try values above 1000.

Presets

Instruments

"Redefine while queued" starts a song with instrument 9, then redefines 9 as a quiet sine; the playing song keeps its sound, and the next click uses the new definition.

Buses

Bus volume follows the effect, so muting a bus also cuts its reverb or echo tail.

Moving the cutoff updates the chain in place: the filter ramps over 20 ms without clicks, and the reverb tail continues.
