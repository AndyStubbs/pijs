Sound Samples

Listening demo for decoded and streamed audio instances: one-shots, loops, playback rate, pan, pause and resume, stream replacement, and mixed synth and sample load. Serve the repository over HTTP (npm run server); audio does not load from file: pages.

One-shots (decoded)

Every instance fades in and out over a few milliseconds, so none should click.

Loop instance (decoded)

Volume, rate, and pan apply live through setAudio, including while delayed or paused. Rate is 2n from 0.0625 to 16. Duration counts every loop pass; 0 loops forever.

Stream (media element)

A second Play fades out the current instance and replaces it. Stream rates are limited to 0.25–4, and timing is approximate.

Mixed load

Loops are protected from stealing: a synth flood keeps them playing, and once 64 loops hold every voice, new sounds and loops are not played.
